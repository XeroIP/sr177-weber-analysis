Executive Summary

What This Report Covers

The expansion of State Route 177 represents a defining shift in the transportation architecture of the Wasatch Front. This report analyzes the planning framework, property impacts, engineering design standards, noise abatement policy, governance actions, and financial timeline for the Weber County extension — with direct attention to properties along the 5100 West and 3800 South corridor in West Haven.

The segment from 1800 North (West Point) to 4000 South (West Haven) is currently in the State Environmental Study (SES) phase. The study corridor runs along or adjacent to 5100 West per UDOT maps. No preferred alignment has been officially selected, and the project remains unfunded for construction. Utah's Unified Transportation Plan places this segment in Phase 3 (2043–2050), though local officials are lobbying for earlier action.

For property owners near 5100 West and 3800 South: your address sits at the eastern boundary of the study corridor. Under a centerline-on-5100W scenario with a 250-foot ROW, east-side properties would be at the ROW edge. If the alignment shifts east, direct property take becomes possible. If it shifts west toward 5500W, sound wall eligibility becomes the primary concern.

Project Timeline

Chronological Progression

January 2024
Phase 1 Opens
16-mile West Davis Highway (Farmington to SR-193 in West Point) opens to traffic. $750M project completed.
November 2025
Weber County SES Scoping Meeting
Public Scoping Meeting held November 20, 2025. SES formally launched for 1800 N → 4000 S segment.
December 2025
Phase 2 Utility Work Begins
Preliminary utility relocations begin in Davis County along SR-193 → 1800 N alignment. Rocky Mountain Power, Hooper Water, Canal Company crews active.
Spring 2026
SES Alternatives Development
Weber County SES moves into Alternatives Development and Evaluation. Build Alternative footprint mapping underway for 5100 West corridor.
Summer 2026
Phase 2 Major Construction Begins
$225M Phase 2 extension (SR-193 → 1800 N) enters full construction. Land clearing, earthmoving, drainage, paving through 2028.
Fall 2026
Draft SES Published
Expected release of Draft SES with alignment maps, resource analysis, and public hearing. First opportunity to see formal ROW boundaries.
Late 2026
Final SES / Record of Decision
SES concludes. If approved, project enters 2-year design phase. No construction funding allocated at this stage.
2028–2029
Phase 2 Completion
Phase 2 scheduled for completion late 2028 to early 2029. SR-177 terminus reaches 1800 North in West Point.
2029–2030 (optimistic)
Design Phase Completion (If Funded)
Following SES approval, 2-year design phase would complete. Earliest theoretical construction start if funding secured rapidly — a scenario not currently supported by the Unified Transportation Plan.
2043–2050 (official)
Phase 3 Construction (Utah Unified Transportation Plan)
Current official plan placement for Weber County SR-177 extension construction. Weber County officials are lobbying to accelerate this window.

Reality check on the accelerated timeline: The accelerated scenario requires UDOT and the Legislature to allocate significant funding — likely hundreds of millions of dollars — to a project not currently in the Transportation Investment Fund (TIF). This would require either federal infrastructure funding, a legislative appropriation, or displacement of other funded projects. Weber County officials are lobbying for this but have not secured commitments as of April 2026.

Analog Comparison

Davis County Precedent Applied to Weber

The Davis County West Davis Corridor provides the best available timeline analog:

  • Environmental Impact Statement completed: 2017
  • Construction funding secured and design finalized: ~2020–2021
  • Construction commenced: Early 2021
  • Open to traffic: January 2024
  • Total: ~7 years from EIS completion to opening

Applying this interval to the Weber County SES completion (late 2026): an equivalent timeline would produce a highway opening around 2033–2034 — but only if the project were funded immediately upon SES completion, which it currently is not. The more realistic scenario under the Unified Transportation Plan is 2043–2050.

## Independent findings

### 1. Phase 2 construction status is confirmed

UDOT's current West Davis page says crews begin preliminary work along 700 South to 1800 North in July 2026. Listed work includes vegetation and brush clearing, construction fencing, environmental mitigation, and site preparation. UDOT anticipates full construction during the first week of August, with pile driving near 700 South among the first major activities.

- **Source strength:** Official government
- **Publishable:** Yes
- **Affected pages:** Timeline, Planning, Engineering, Property Owner Guide, Hooper Community
- **Page-content date:** July 2026; first week of August 2026 for anticipated full construction
- **Limitation:** The homepage update is not date-stamped, so use "verified July 22, 2026."

### 2. The Weber County SES remains in progress

No Draft SES, preferred alternative, final alignment, or parcel-level right-of-way determination was found on UDOT's study page. The site's core warnings should remain unchanged.

- **Source strength:** Official government
- **Publishable:** Yes
- **Affected pages:** Every page with an `as of May 23, 2026` study-status statement
- **Page-content date:** As of July 22, 2026
- **Limitation:** Absence from the controlling project page supports a status statement, not a claim that no internal design work exists.

### 3. WFRC public comment is active and time-sensitive

WFRC released the Draft 2027-2032 TIP on July 6, 2026. Comments close August 2. The Ogden open house is July 28 from 4:30 to 7:00 p.m. at Lindquist Field, 2330 Lincoln Avenue. The announcement includes the West Davis extension from SR-193 to 1800 North.

- **Source strength:** Official government
- **Publishable:** Yes
- **Affected pages:** Governance, Timeline, Project Status
- **Page-content dates:** July 6 through August 2, 2026; July 28, 2026
- **Limitation:** The announcement does not say the 1800 North and 4500 West intersection project is in the released draft.

### 4. The 1800 North and 4500 West project remains a submission, not a verified draft-TIP project

The May 28 WFRC packet documents the UDOT project submission and its warning that the intersection would fail upon SR-177 opening without improvements. The released TIP announcement does not list this intersection project, and the interactive comment map was not inspectable through a text fetch.

- **Source strength:** Official government
- **Publishable:** Yes, only as a submission
- **Affected pages:** Governance or Timeline
- **Page-content date:** May 28, 2026
- **Not safe to publish:** That the project was selected for or appears in the released Draft 2027-2032 TIP

### 5. The Phase 2 completion schedule conflicts within official sources

The strongest current UDOT announcement, dated March 26, 2026, says construction is anticipated to last through mid-2029. An older UDOT project-preview page says summer 2028, and WFRC's July 6 announcement also says opening is anticipated in 2028. The Standard-Examiner citation remains contextual but was bot-blocked during this independent pass.

The site should not describe this as only a media-versus-UDOT conflict.

- **Recommended wording:** "UDOT's March 26, 2026 construction announcement says work is anticipated through mid-2029. An older UDOT project preview and WFRC's July 2026 summary refer to 2028."
- **Source strength:** Official government plus professional news
- **Publishable:** Yes, as a clearly attributed conflict
- **Affected pages:** Engineering, Timeline, Hooper Community
- **Current schedule to lead with:** Mid-2029, because it comes from UDOT's specific March 26 construction announcement

### 6. Weber County's west-of-river action remains publishable with limits

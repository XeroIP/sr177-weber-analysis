## Search methodology

Live web fetches and searches (Claude Code WebFetch/WebSearch) across official, regional-planning, local-government, media, and contractor sources. ArcGIS item metadata verified against **raw JSON with programmatic epoch conversion** (curl + Python `datetime.fromtimestamp`), per the methodology note in the 2026-07-07 findings — AI-summarized fetches have previously mis-converted these timestamps. The MapServer failure was confirmed with two raw curl attempts (base service and `/layers` path). PDF liveness confirmed with an HTTP range request.

Weber County Commission work-session minutes were probed directly at their predictable URL pattern (`webercountyutah.gov/commission/documents/work_session/min_pdf/min_MMDDYYYY.pdf`) — these are not reliably indexed by search engines and do not appear on the county news page. Large Planning Commission packets (600+ pages) were downloaded and keyword-scanned programmatically (pypdf). Note that the county's own records name the corridor **"West Weber Freeway"** and **"North Legacy Highway"** rather than SR-177 / West Davis — search plans must include those terms to surface county governance actions.

## Methodology note (for future passes)

- Verify all ArcGIS timestamps against raw JSON with programmatic epoch conversion — AI-summarized fetches have mis-converted them before. The `utility.arcgis.com/usrsvcs` proxy endpoint can fail wholesale (HTTP 500 token errors) — when it does, the web map item's `/data?f=json` endpoint still exposes the operational-layer inventory and is the fallback verification path.
- `westdavis.udot.utah.gov` has no dated news permalinks (`/news/` 404s); homepage updates must be cited with a "verified" date.
- **Corridor alt-names:** Weber County records call this corridor **"West Weber Freeway"** and **"North Legacy Highway"** (historically "North Legacy Corridor," per the county's 2009 supplemental study). SR-177 / West Davis keyword sweeps will miss county governance actions — add these names to every future search plan.
- **Weber County work-session minutes** live at a predictable URL: `webercountyutah.gov/commission/documents/work_session/min_pdf/min_MMDDYYYY.pdf` — probe recent Mondays directly; they are not reliably indexed by search engines and the county news page does not announce them.
- **PMN planning packets** can be huge (this one: 666 pages, 17 MB) and duplicated under multiple file IDs; keyword-scan programmatically.
- **QuorumScribe** (`quorumscribe.com`, formerly publicmeetings.org) publishes **AI-generated** summaries of Utah local-government meeting videos (self-disclosed on its homepage, verified 2026-07-21). Useful for locating which meeting discussed a topic; never citable — the publication rules exclude AI-generated summaries as sources. Verify against official minutes or the meeting recording. `citizenportal.ai` is the same class.
- **Cross-verification of the independent pass (2026-07-22):** an independent AI research pass (`docs/research/findings/2026-07-22.md`) was reconciled into this record. Every new claim was re-verified live before integration. Verified and integrated: the UDOT project-preview page (intra-official 2028/mid-2029 conflict), the standalone approved March 3 minutes URL, Granite's Wells Pit quantities, ExUTIL service URLs, ROW item snippet/tags, and the Ogden open-house address.

## Executive summary

1. **UDOT has set firm dates for the Draft SES and public hearing.** This supersedes the site's "expected Fall 2026" language. *(Official record)*

   | Item | Date and details |
   |---|---|
   | Draft SES online | Nov 6, 2026 |
   | Formal comment period | Nov 6 – Dec 11, 2026 |
   | Online informational presentation | Mon, Nov 9, 2026, 6–7 p.m. Register at udotinput.utah.gov/sr177extension to get the link. |
   | Official in-person public hearing | Tue, Nov 10, 2026, 4:30–7:30 p.m., at Roy Junior High School (5347 South 2100 West, Roy). Open-house format with no formal presentation. |
   | Neighborhood meetings | Invitation-only meetings in early November, "for property owners and tenants affected by planned impacts" |
   | Final SES | Late 2026 / Early 2027 |

   - UDOT states that the Draft SES "identifies a Preferred Alternative."
   - The study page now dates Alternatives Development and Evaluation to "Early 2026–Summer 2026" and describes it as "Development of the Preferred Alternative."
   - The Preferred Alternative itself has **not** been published as of Oct 28, 2026.

2. **New official detail on competing northern (Phase 4) alignments.** Draft Jun 9, 2026 Weber County Planning Commission work-session minutes were first posted in the Oct 13, 2026 packet. They describe a "West Weber Freeway Corridor" outreach session. *(Official record, draft minutes)*
   - **Alignments presented:**
     - the current general-plan alignment, with two corridor options including one along 8300 West;
     - the county's preferred "green" alignment, adopted in 2009;
     - an alternate "red" alignment further west;
     - "two additional UDOT alternatives — 'blue' and 'purple' — located further east, in the range of 4700 to 8000 West."
   - **UDOT's position:** staff said UDOT "has not yet communicated a definitive preferred alignment and has not been responsive to County meeting invitations."
   - **Outcome:** the Planning Commission reached "unanimous consensus in support of the more westerly corridor alignment." The desired outcome was a formal letter to UDOT signed by all three County Commissioners, all seven Planning Commissioners, landowners and developers.
   - **WFRC comment:** a WFRC staff member said UDOT has been cautious about alignments that may face wetland or Army Corps permitting challenges, and that "legislative support for these state-funded freeway facilities has been growing."
   - **Inland Port:** the Port Authority said it is "not formally advocating for a specific alignment."

3. **County general plan amendment (GPA2026-01).**
   - On Aug 11, 2026 the Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation, 6-0, on future land-use map changes. In those changes, "the proposed West Weber corridor road alignment was also revised to route around the state property and follow higher, drier ground." *(Official record, draft minutes, Oct 13 packet)*
   - On Sep 8, 2026 the Planning Commission forwarded a 5-0 positive recommendation on the plan consolidation. *(Official record, draft minutes)*
   - The County Commission's Sep 22, 2026 agenda included a resolution to approve the Consolidated Weber County General Plan. The resolution packet's plan text calls the corridor "State Route 177 Corridor (Northern Extension of West Davis Highway)." *(Official record)*
   - **Adoption is not confirmed.** County Commission minutes for Sep 22 onward were not posted as of Oct 28.

4. **Speaker Mike Schultz addressed the Hooper City Council on Sep 17, 2026.** It was a listed agenda item. *(Official record: agenda)*
   - AI-generated meeting summaries (citizenportal.ai), and an Oct 7 Hoodline article citing them, report that he said state funding extends SR-177 only to about 5500 South in the near term and that extension to 4000 South is unlikely in the next few years. *(Lead)*
   - No UDOT, STIP or legislative record confirming a 5500 South funding limit was found.
   - **Not safe to publish as a funding fact.**

5. **No relevant change** in West Haven council records (Sep 16 and Oct 7 minutes; Oct 21 agenda and packet), Hooper's Oct 1 packet (the Oct 15 meeting was cancelled), the HDR "SR-177 Level 2 Screening" map (unchanged since Sep 8), or WFRC's RTP schedule (2027–2055 RTP adoption slated for May 2027).
   - The Horrocks right-of-way feature service `UT_11199_SR_177_AGOL` was modified Oct 26, 2026. Metadata only; no parcel contents were reviewed.

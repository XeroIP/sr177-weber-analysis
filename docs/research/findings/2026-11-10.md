## Executive summary

1. **The Draft SES is out, and it names a Preferred Alternative.** UDOT posted it on Nov 6, 2026 in three files:
   - Volume 1, 123 pages;
   - Volume 2, the mapbooks, 38 pages;
   - an Appendix of 1,159 pages.

   The Preferred Alternative is the "SR-177 Extension Build Alternative." *(Official record, draft)*
   - **Road:** a new four-lane freeway on the 5100 West corridor, about 3.5 miles, from 1800 North in West Point to 4000 South in West Haven.
   - **Interchanges:** diamond interchanges at 1800 North and 5500 South (SR-97), and a single-point urban interchange (SPUI) at 4000 South.
   - **Alignment:** a "hybrid." It runs west of 5100 West in the south and crosses to the east side after the 5500 South interchange.
   - **Local roads:** 5100 West stays as a frontage road between about 6000 S and 5500 S, and between 5100 S and 4000 S. 5300 West is extended from 5100 S to 5900 S. Several side streets become cul-de-sacs or are removed; see N1.
   - **Trails:** a 12-foot shared-use path on the west side. Grade-separated crossings of 1800 N, 5500 S and 4000 S, and an underpass for the planned Hooper Slough Trail.

2. **Impacts in the Draft SES.** *(Official record, draft)*
   - **Relocations: the document disagrees with itself.**
     - Summary table and Section 3.4: 73 residential properties and three commercial properties (up to six businesses).
     - Section 3.6, the only page footered "November 2026": 77 residential properties.
     - Both give ten full acquisitions of undeveloped land.
   - **Acreage, which also differs between sections:**
     - Summary table: about 129.6 acres of full acquisition, and partial acquisitions from 116 parcels totaling about 47.9 acres.
     - Section 3.6: about 138.88 acres from 90 parcels in full, and about 48.11 acres from 120 parcels in part.
   - **Noise:** 324 of 620 noise-sensitive receivers would be impacted, and 234 of those would see a substantial (≥10 dBA) increase.
     - Fifteen walls were evaluated. Three are recommended for final design and balloting.
     - The 4000 South interchange was **not** included in the noise analysis. UDOT says it will reanalyze that later.
   - **Wetlands and waters:** about 0.433 acres of wetlands and 3.253 acres (14,320 linear feet) of other potential waters of the U.S.
   - **Historic properties:** an adverse effect on three NRHP-eligible houses, all full acquisitions. Mitigation is through an MOA with SHPO, which concurred in October 2026.
   - **Other:**
     - about 2.4 acres of one Agricultural Protection Area;
     - 33 water-right points of diversion near the footprint;
     - about 65 acres of new impervious surface;
     - about 106 acres converted to transportation use.

3. **Phasing and funding.** *(Official record, draft)*
   - The Draft SES says construction "could be constructed in the following two phases as funding becomes available":
     - Phase 1 — the 1800 North interchange and the extension to 5500 South;
     - Phase 2 — the 5500 South interchange and the extension to 4000 South.
   - The 4000 South SPUI "would be reevaluated and constructed as part of a future UDOT project."
   - No cost estimate and no funding commitment appear in the SES.
   - The SES's traffic analysis uses 2032 as "Opening Day." This is an analysis assumption, not a construction schedule.

4. **The site's "Phase 3 (2043–2050)" construction window is wrong for this segment.**
   - The Draft SES states that WFRC's 2023–2050 RTP lists both pieces of the 1800 North → 4000 South extension as Phase 2 (2033–2042) new construction:
     - W-D-2023-R-4, 1800 N to the county line;
     - W-W-2023-R-71, the county line to 4000 S.
   - It repeats this in the air-quality section ("identified in the WFRC RTP ... for Phase 2").
   - This settles the conflict flagged on 2026-09-27. *(Official record)*
   - RTP phases are "assumed funding" windows in a fiscally constrained plan, not funding commitments.
   - UDOT's **Draft STIP 2027–2032** has no construction line for 1800 N → 5500 S or → 4000 S. It was approved for public comment, and the report ran Aug 6, 2026. *(Official record, draft program)*
     - PIN 21158, the SES, at $5M.
     - PIN 20927, SR-193 → 1800 N, about $270M and under construction.

5. **UDOT has programmed a corridor study for the northern (Phase 4) segment.** *(Official record; overlap-period, not previously found)*
   - **WFRC Transportation Coordinating Committee, Nov 20, 2025 packet.** A UDOT project slide describes the study:
     - "Re-evaluate information presented in 2009 North Legacy Transportation Study. The study will look at the SR-177 alignment from 4000S. North to Box Elder County The Study will also look at the Northern connection to I-15 in Box Elder County";
     - "Early 2026 to Begin Study";
     - programmed amount $200K.
     - The slide's heading reads "SR-177; 1800 N to 4000 S," which looks like a template carry-over. The body and map describe the northern segment.
   - **UDOT Draft STIP 2027–2032 (Aug 2026)** lists PIN 22829, "SR-177 Corridor Study; 4000 S to Box Elder County," status "Proposed," labeled "SR-177 West Weber Corridor." No amount is shown.
   - This is a corridor study, not an environmental study. The site's "UDOT has not announced a Phase 4 study" wording needs correction.
   - **Inference:** HDR's "SR177 Phase 4" / "Level 2 Screening" working map (Aug–Sep 2026) is probably part of this study. Not stated by any source.

## New findings (detail)

### N1. Preferred Alternative described (Draft SES Vol 1, ES.5 and Ch. 2)

The SR-177 Extension Build Alternative is identified as the Preferred Alternative "because it meets the purpose and need for the project." It comprises:

- **Freeway and interchanges**
  - a new four-lane freeway, 1800 North to 4000 South;
  - new diamond interchanges at 1800 North and 5500 South;
  - a SPUI at 4000 South.
- **New and kept roads**
  - 5300 West extended from 5100 South to 5900 South;
  - 5100 West kept as a frontage road between about 6000 S and 5500 S, and between 5100 S and 4000 S;
  - east–west access kept on 2425 North and on 5100 South, each by an underpass;
  - 5050 West extended south from 5000 South to 5100 South.
- **Removed roads and intersections**
  - 5900 South and 5725 South removed between about 5150 W and 5100 W;
  - the 5300 South / 5100 West intersection removed, with a cul-de-sac west of the canal;
  - 4825 South and 4725 South removed between 5050 W and 5100 W;
  - the 4400 S, 4250 S and 4175 S intersections with 5100 W removed, with cul-de-sacs near 5050 W;
  - 4075 South removed between 5025 W and 5100 W.
- **Trails and crossings**
  - a shared-use path from the Davis County Drain to 4000 South, on the west side;
  - grade-separated crossings of 1800 N (east side), 5500 S (west side) and 4000 S (west side);
  - a pedestrian underpass for the planned Hooper Slough Trail.

Site pages affected: Overview, Project Status, Planning, Engineering, Hooper, West Haven, Property, Property Owner Guide, Timeline.

### N2. How the alignment was chosen (Ch. 2; Appendix Concept Development Memo, May 2026)

The SES cites the 2001 North Legacy study, which chose "Sub-alternative B" along the east side of 5100 W, and the 2009 supplemental study, which reviewed 23 alignments plus three broad corridors including 3100 W and a corridor west of 5100 W. It says "UDOT has consistently supported the 5100 West alignment through corridor preservation."

The memo compares three concepts, all on 5100 West:

| Measure | East Shift | West Shift | Hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full acquisitions/relocations | 89 | 91 | 84 |
| Potential additional relocations | 12 | 7 | 14 |
| Partial acquisitions | 94 | 69 | 82 |
| UDOT-owned parcels used | 39 | 20 | 33 |
| Aquatic resources (acres) | 8.0 | 6.7 | 6.6 |
| APAs (acres) | 1.7 | 7.9 | 1.0 |
| Potentially eligible historic structures | 18 | 17 | 19 |

The hybrid was selected because it "minimized the number of residential and commercial relocations" and minimized archaeological, aquatic and agricultural impacts. These are concept-level counts; the SES's later figures differ (N3).

### N3. Impacts (Draft SES Table ES.1 and Ch. 3)

- **Right-of-way: conflict inside the document.** See the executive summary. Report both figures; do not choose one.
- **Businesses:** six relocated, five of them in one plaza near 4000 South and 5100 West plus a storage business. Partial acquisitions from three more, including Crane Field Golf Course. The SES names the businesses; the site will give counts only.
- **Noise (Section 3.9; Appendix Noise Study):**
  - 324 of 620 receivers impacted, 234 with a substantial increase.
  - Recommended walls:
    - Wall 10: east side, 5500 S to 4600 S, about 5,400 ft by 17 ft.
    - Wall 13: east side, 4400 S to 4000 S, about 2,467 ft by 16 ft.
    - Wall 14: south side of 4000 S between SR-177 and the Wilson Canal, about 991 ft by 6 ft.
  - All walls are "still subject to final design and balloting."
  - The SPUI at 4000 South was not evaluated "due to anticipated construction phasing"; it will be reanalyzed in a future phase.
- **Wetlands/WOTUS:** 0.433 ac of wetlands and 3.253 ac (14,320 lf) of other potential WOTUS. A CWA Section 404 permit is anticipated.
- **Cultural:**
  - three archaeological canals with no adverse effect;
  - three houses with an adverse effect (full acquisitions);
  - two houses with no adverse effect from partial acquisitions;
  - one house with no impact.
  - The MOA with SHPO covers intensive-level survey forms and local history research. Addresses are in the SES and are not repeated on the site.
- **Farmland:** 2.4 ac of the Rawson and Moyle Families APA at 4000 S / 5100 W. The remaining Century Farm parcel is not affected.
- **Water rights:** 33 points of diversion. Mitigation options are relocating the well or diversion, or abandoning it and compensating the owner.
- **Air quality:** no adverse effect expected. The project is in the conforming RTP (Phase 2).
